Privacy Policy

DailyStamp · Last updated: November 4, 2026

DailyStamp collects nothing. Your photos and records stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and your location goes to Apple only to look up a place name.

Data stored on your device

DailyStamp runs no servers. Photos, videos, and their records are stored only inside the app on your device. Deleting a record deletes its files; deleting the app removes all of its on-device data.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

There are no logins, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising of any kind. DailyStamp does not collect email addresses, identifiers, usage events, or any other information about you.

Location

With your permission, your location is used to stamp a place name on photos and to write standard GPS EXIF data into them. To find the place name, the app sends the coordinates to Apple’s location service when you take a photo and when you import a photo that carries a location. Apple handles that request; the coordinates are not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see them. If you turn the place stamp off, the app stops using your location and sends nothing to Apple, and the GPS data is stripped from the photo as well.

iCloud backup

If enabled, records are copied to your own private iCloud container so they can be restored on a new device. This storage belongs to your Apple account; we cannot access it. You can turn the backup off at any time in Settings.

Photo library and microphone

Saving to your photo library uses add-only permission — DailyStamp cannot read your library. Importing photos uses the system picker, which shares only the photos you choose. The microphone is used only while you record video.

Payments

The optional DailyStamp Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store payment details.

Children's privacy

DailyStamp is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
