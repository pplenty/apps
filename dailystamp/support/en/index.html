Frequently asked questions

Why is the place sometimes missing from the stamp?

DailyStamp only stamps a place it can trust. Indoors or underground, the location can be too imprecise — a wrong address is worse than none, so the stamp is left without one. A small note above the shutter tells you why while it happens.

Where are my photos stored?

Inside the app, organized by date. They also appear in the Files app under readable date names, and you can export any photo from its detail view. Saving copies to the Photos app is optional in Settings.

I switched phones. How do I get my records back?

Keep iCloud backup on (it is on by default), sign in to the same Apple account on the new phone, and install DailyStamp. Your records restore automatically on first launch.

Does turning off the place stamp really remove GPS from the photo?

Yes. The stamp text and the photo's GPS EXIF data are removed together, so sharing the photo cannot reveal where it was taken.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Purchases follow your Apple account, and Family Sharing is supported.

Why can't the shutter sound be muted?

On devices sold in some regions (such as Korea and Japan), the system enforces the shutter sound and apps cannot bypass it.
