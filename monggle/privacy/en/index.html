Privacy Policy

Monggle · Last updated: July 31, 2026

Monggle does not collect personal information. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

Monggle does not collect names, email addresses, locations, contacts, or any other personal information. There are no accounts or logins, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

The quotes in your garden, paper themes, reminder times, and other preferences are stored on the device where the app is installed. Monggle runs no servers and cannot transmit or view this data. Deleting the app deletes your preferences; the quotes in your garden stay in the iCloud backup below.

iCloud backup

So that changing phones or reinstalling the app doesn’t lose the collection you built, the quotes in your garden are also kept in your own iCloud key-value store. It holds nothing but a list of quote identifiers, lives only in your Apple account, and the developer cannot access it. Turning Monggle off under iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud stops this backup.

Notifications

Monggle uses iOS local notifications only when you choose to enable them. Notification content and schedules are never sent to an external server.

Payments

Optional tips are processed by Apple through App Store StoreKit. Monggle never sees or stores card or payment details.

Children's privacy

Monggle is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
