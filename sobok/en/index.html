Write as much as you like

Nothing to say,
or a lot to say

On days with nothing to say, one tap on a feeling keeps today. On days you want to write, a title, long writing, photos and notes on the book you just read or the movie you just saw all fit in one entry.

♡

Today's feeling, in one tap

Tap one of seven feeling faces and it becomes today's entry. When there is something to say, add a line to that same entry.

✎

Books and movies too

A title, long writing, photos and a rating — add only what you want that day. Search for the book you just read or the movie you just saw, or type it in yourself.

❅

Days that pile up softly

Each day you write, its feeling settles on the calendar, and days in a row drift together like snow. See at a glance which weeks were good and which were hard.

Inside the app

Every day you keep piles up softly

“Sobok-sobok” is the Korean sound of snow quietly piling up — the way your days gather here, one by one.

No account.
Your diary stays on your phone.

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your diary is stored only on your device, and you can export every entry and photo as a ZIP file and bring it back on a new device. Writing, search, calendar, app lock and backup are all free; only Sobok Plus, which binds your entries into a PDF diary book, is a one-time purchase.
