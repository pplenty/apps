Privacy Policy

Sobok · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Sobok has no accounts. Your diary, feelings, photos and notes on books and movies are never sent to or collected by the developer.

What is stored on your device

  • Entry titles and text
  • The feeling, date and rating you chose, and whether an entry is a favorite
  • App-only copies of the photos you picked
  • Title, creator, year and link of the books and movies you picked
  • An unfinished draft, and whether the app lock is on
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, and its time
  • Whether Sobok Plus was purchased, and when that was last confirmed

This is kept in an SQLite database, the app's documents folder and the system preferences on your device. Deleting the app may delete this data too, depending on the operating system, so export a backup first if you need one.

Operating system backups

Depending on your iOS or Android backup settings, app data may be included in the encrypted device backups or device-to-device transfers the operating system provides. Sobok does not receive or read these backups on any server; whether they include the app and how long they are kept follow your system account and device settings.

Purchases

Sobok Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; the app keeps only whether it was purchased and when that was last confirmed, on the device. The developer receives no payment details or purchase history.

Network use

Only when you search for a book or movie is the search term sent to the Wikidata Query Service. Sobok does not record search terms, results or how you use the app on any server. Apart from that search and store payments the app does not use the network, and it never sends diary content.

Permissions

  • Photo library: only when you attach photos you picked to an entry.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode: only to authenticate on the device when you turn on the app lock.
  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on the device without any server or push service, and never contain diary content.
  • Internet: only when you search for a book or movie.

Export

On request, the app makes a ZIP file of all entries and their photos. You choose where it is saved or sent in the system share sheet.

Third-party SDKs

No advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting SDKs. Book and movie search uses the Wikidata Query Service run by the Wikimedia Foundation, called without an account or API key.

Children

Sobok is not directed at children and collects no personal information, including age.

Contact

Questions about privacy: sobok@jdgrid.com.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top is updated, and any change to what is collected or sent is also announced in the app update notes.
