Where is my diary stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server, so the developer cannot see your diary. See the privacy policy.

Will my entries move to a new phone?

They move with the operating system's device backup or transfer when that includes app data. To be sure, make a ZIP backup first with Export a backup in Settings. The diary.json inside is human-readable.

Can I bring a backup back?

Yes. Pick the exported ZIP in Import a backup in Settings. Importing merges: nothing on the device is deleted, the more recently edited copy of an entry wins, entries only in the backup are added, and photos come back too.

What happens if I delete the app?

Depending on the operating system, app data may be deleted with it — export a backup first. Once your diary grows, Settings tells you how many entries you have written since your last backup.

A book or movie doesn't show up in search

Search needs an internet connection. If the work isn't there, add its title and creator yourself from the search screen.

The app lock won't turn on

Your device needs Face ID, a fingerprint or a screen-lock passcode set up first.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sobok Plus stays with your Apple or Google account. On a device signed in to the same account, tap Restore purchase in Settings.
